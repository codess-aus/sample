# AI for Leaders — Let's Demystify AI

AI is reshaping industries, disrupting established business models and creating
new competitive pressures. Executives and senior leaders who understand what AI
actually is — rather than what the hype says it is — are the ones best positioned
to make decisions that create lasting value.

This workshop cuts through the noise.

---

## Who This Workshop Is For

This workshop is designed for executives, senior managers and board members who:

- Need to make strategic decisions about AI but lack a technical background
- Want to evaluate AI proposals with confidence rather than relying entirely on vendors
- Are responsible for governance, risk or accountability in their organisations
- Are leading or sponsoring an AI adoption programme
- Want to understand what their technical teams are actually doing

No prior technical knowledge is required. The workshop is deliberately designed
for decision-makers, not data scientists.

---

## The Challenges This Workshop Addresses

Leaders routinely encounter situations where AI raises difficult questions:

- A vendor proposes an AI solution — how do you evaluate it?
- Your organisation wants to adopt AI at scale — where do you start?
- Your team produces results from an AI model — how do you know if they are reliable?
- You are asked to approve an AI governance framework — what should it contain?
- Your competitors are moving fast — how do you move deliberately without falling behind?

This workshop builds the understanding and vocabulary needed to engage with these
questions confidently.

---

## What Participants Will Learn

By the end of the workshop, participants will be able to:

- Explain what AI is and is not, in plain language
- Identify where AI can and cannot create genuine business value
- Ask the right questions when evaluating AI proposals
- Understand the conditions required for AI to scale successfully
- Apply a practical framework for AI governance and responsible use
- Recognise the risks of AI adoption and how to manage them
- Lead organisational change in an AI-enabled environment
- Establish meaningful measures of AI value

---

## Workshop Topics

=== "Strategy & Value"

    - **AI strategy**: Aligning AI initiatives with organisational goals
    - **Identifying valuable use cases**: How to separate genuine opportunities from noise
    - **From experimentation to capability**: Moving beyond pilots to sustainable AI
    - **Measuring value**: Defining and tracking what success actually looks like

=== "Scale & Governance"

    - **Scalable AI**: The conditions that allow AI to grow safely and effectively
    - **Governance frameworks**: Roles, responsibilities and decision rights
    - **Risk and accountability**: Understanding and managing what can go wrong
    - **Responsible AI**: Fairness, transparency, privacy and the limits of automation

=== "People & Change"

    - **Adoption**: Why AI initiatives fail and how to avoid common traps
    - **Organisational readiness**: Assessing your team, data and infrastructure
    - **Change management**: Bringing people along rather than imposing change
    - **Building internal capability**: Moving from dependence to competence

---

## Practical Exercises

The workshop is interactive and discussion-led. Typical activities include:

- **Use-case evaluation**: Assessing whether a proposed AI application is worth pursuing
- **Governance design**: Drafting the key elements of an AI governance framework
- **Risk mapping**: Identifying and prioritising AI-related risks in a given scenario
- **Value measurement**: Defining meaningful success metrics for an AI initiative
- **Stakeholder simulation**: Practising how to explain AI decisions to a non-technical audience

---

## Expected Outcomes

Participants leave the workshop with:

!!! success "What you will take away"

    - A clear, practical mental model for understanding AI
    - A framework for evaluating AI proposals and vendor claims
    - A starting structure for an AI governance approach
    - Practical tools for measuring and communicating AI value
    - Confidence to lead conversations about AI with technical and non-technical stakeholders

## Customisation

Every organisation is different. This workshop can be adapted to:

- Your industry sector and competitive context
- Current AI initiatives your organisation is running
- Specific governance or risk frameworks you are working within
- The mix of technical and non-technical participants
- The level of AI maturity in your organisation

A short discovery conversation before the workshop ensures the content is
directly relevant to your situation.
