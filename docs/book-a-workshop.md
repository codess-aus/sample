# Bring Practical AI Learning to Your Organisation

Whether you are running a leadership retreat, a student programme, a university
module or a professional development day, these workshops can be tailored to fit.

Two workshops are available:

- **AI for Leaders — Let's Demystify AI**: For executives, senior managers and
  board members who need to make confident decisions about AI.
- **Industrial Problem-Solving Using AI & Decision Sciences**: For high school
  students, university students and early-career professionals developing
  structured problem-solving and analytical skills.

Both workshops are available in-person, virtually, or in a hybrid format, and
can be scaled from a single session to a multi-day programme.

---
