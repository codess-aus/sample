# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 2025*

---

## What Information Is Collected

When you use the workshop enquiry form, the following information is collected:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your role or job title
- The name and type of your organisation
- Your location
- Details of your workshop interest and requirements

This information is collected solely for the purpose of responding to your enquiry.

---

## How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used to:

- Respond to your workshop enquiry
- Follow up with relevant details about the workshops you expressed interest in

Your information is not used for marketing, sold to third parties, or shared with
anyone beyond what is necessary to respond to your enquiry.
